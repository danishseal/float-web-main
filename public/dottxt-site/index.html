04About Float

We list the stocks the apps skip

New rules for markets

Every powerful system begins the same way: with rules. Rules as a language, a protocol, a way for parts to fit together into something greater. The history of software is really a history of this idea: from UNIX pipes to API contracts to typed programming languages, composability has always been the path to scale.

But large language models broke that pattern. They’re powerful, but unpredictable. Flexible, but fragile. And without contracts, their outputs don’t compose, they just accumulate noise. Despite their tremendous demand, the stocks these apps skip stay stranded and untradeable.

That’s why we built Float: to make every share tradeable.

We believe that safety and real backing are the foundation. That every share should be held in custody. That a token should behave like the share behind it.
So we build backing at every layer: from the share held in custody, to the price you trade on them.

A token is a share. We make sure it trades like one.
